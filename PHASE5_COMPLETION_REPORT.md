# Phase 5: Advanced Cardiac & Hemodynamics - Completion Report

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Total Units Generated:** 274  
**Cumulative Total (Phases 1-5):** 1,321 units

## Module Breakdown

### 1. ACLS & Resuscitation (57 units)
**Coverage:** BLS vs ACLS algorithms, shockable vs non-shockable rhythms, VF/VT/asystole/PEA management, medication timing and dosing, post-cardiac arrest care, TTM, prognostication, special resuscitations (pregnancy, hypothermia, PE, poisoning), team dynamics, when to stop resuscitation, ECPR indications and technique.

**Key Topics:**
- Chain of survival and time-dependent outcomes
- High-quality CPR components (rate, depth, recoil)
- Epinephrine, amiodarone, atropine, sodium bicarbonate, calcium
- ROSC recognition and post-arrest management
- Neurologic prognostication at 72h
- Perimortem cesarean delivery
- Esophageal intubation recognition
- Medication routes (IV, IO, ETT)

### 2. Advanced Arrhythmia Management (66 units)
**Coverage:** Bradycardia (sinus, AV blocks I/II/III), atropine indications and mechanism, transcutaneous/transvenous pacing, narrow-complex tachycardia (SVT, AVNRT, AVRT, WPW, atrial flutter, MAT, atrial tachycardia, junctional tachycardia), vagal maneuvers, adenosine, verapamil/diltiazem/beta-blockers, atrial fibrillation (rate vs rhythm control, anticoagulation, CHA2DS2-VASc), wide-complex tachycardia (VT vs SVT with aberrancy), torsades de pointes, Long QT syndrome, Brugada syndrome, all antiarrhythmic classes, radiofrequency ablation, ICD, permanent pacemaker, CRT.

**Key Topics:**
- AVNRT reentry physiology
- WPW delta wave recognition
- Early repolarization and J waves
- Digoxin toxicity presentations
- Amiodarone side effects and monitoring
- QT prolongation risk factors
- Carotid sinus hypersensitivity
- Syncope workup and risk stratification

### 3. Invasive Hemodynamic Monitoring (18 units)
**Coverage:** CVP interpretation and waveforms, Swan-Ganz catheter (PAWP, CO measurement), cardiac output calculation, thermodilution method, Fick equation, SVR/PVR calculations, diastolic dysfunction patterns, tamponade physiology, constrictive pericarditis, non-invasive monitoring (echocardiography, NICOM, bioimpedance).

**Key Topics:**
- Normal CVP values and abnormal patterns
- PAWP and LV preload
- CO measurements and trending
- SVR in different shock states
- RV assessment
- Diastolic function assessment

### 4. Shock Physiology Deep Dive (44 units)
**Coverage:** Cardiogenic shock (EF <40%, acute MI, mechanical complications MR/VSD/rupture), RV infarction physiology, hypovolemic shock (hemorrhage, classification, endpoints), distributive shock (septic shock, SIRS/sepsis definitions, source control, immunotherapy), anaphylactic shock (IgE-mediated, epinephrine, treatment), obstructive shock (PE, tamponade, tension pneumothorax, aortic dissection), mixed shock, lactate as prognostic marker, microcirculatory dysfunction in sepsis, refractory shock management.

**Key Topics:**
- Cardiogenic shock algorithm
- RV infarction fluid management
- Hemorrhage classification and damage control
- Septic shock pathophysiology
- Anaphylaxis rapid management
- Lactic acidosis type A vs B
- Adrenal insufficiency in critical illness
- Ischemic bowel complications

### 5. Inotropes, Vasopressors, Vasodilators Deep Dive (43 units)
**Coverage:** Dopamine (dose-dependent effects, renal dose myth), dobutamine (mechanism, indications), milrinone (lusitropy, combination therapy), norepinephrine (first-line status, dosing), epinephrine (mechanism, refractory shock), phenylephrine (pure alpha), vasopressin (second-line, limitations), levosimendan (Europe/Asia, novel mechanism), low-dose hydrocortisone in refractory shock, tachyphylaxis mechanisms, multi-agent hemodynamic support.

**Key Topics:**
- Dose-dependent dopamine effects
- Renal dose dopamine controversy
- Milrinone lusitrophic benefits
- Norepinephrine first-line recommendation
- Stress-induced cardiomyopathy from vasopressors
- Lusitropic effects in diastolic HF
- Drug combinations and escalation strategies

### 6. Advanced Ventilation & ARDS Management (60 units)
**Coverage:** Volume-controlled vs pressure-controlled ventilation, CPAP/BiPAP/PSV, HFOV (high-frequency oscillatory), APRV (airway pressure release), inverse ratio ventilation, PEEP physiology and recruitment, auto-PEEP in asthma/COPD, ARDS management strategy, driving pressure optimization, prone positioning, permissive hypercapnia, special populations (COPD, asthma, obesity), spontaneous breathing trials, weaning protocols and predictors, structured weaning, reintubation prevention.

**Key Topics:**
- Lung-protective ventilation (6-8 mL/kg)
- Driving pressure <15 cm H2O target
- PEEP optimization for recruitment
- Prone positioning in severe ARDS
- IBW calculation for tidal volume
- Volutrauma and barotrauma prevention
- Negative pressure pulmonary edema
- Tracheostomy timing and complications
- High-flow nasal cannula post-extubation

### 7. Mechanical Circulatory Support & ECMO (41 units)
**Coverage:** IABP (mechanism, indications, limitations, complications), Impella (higher flow), TandemHeart (percutaneous VAD), ECMO overview (VA-ECMO vs VV-ECMO), VA-ECMO indications and management, VV-ECMO for ARDS, ECMO cannulation strategies, anticoagulation management, complications (bleeding, hemolysis, thrombosis, infection), weaning from ECMO, ECPR (extracorporeal CPR), VAD (implanted, destination therapy), RV failure post-LVAD.

**Key Topics:**
- IABP diastolic augmentation
- Impella superior to IABP
- VA-ECMO cardiogenic shock support
- VV-ECMO severe ARDS refractory support
- ECMO complications and management
- ECPR in refractory arrest
- Weaning and decannulation decisions
- Destination therapy with LVAD

### 8. Cardiac Imaging & Echocardiography (52 units)
**Coverage:** TTE vs TEE indications, EF estimation (visual, fractional shortening, quantitative), segmental wall motion abnormality patterns (LAD, RCA, LCx territories), diastolic function assessment (E/A ratio, tissue Doppler, E/e' ratio), mitral valve assessment (stenosis, regurgitation, mechanisms), aortic valve (AS, AR, calcification), tricuspid/pulmonary valves, pulmonary hypertension assessment via TR jet, pericardial effusion and tamponade, acute mitral regurgitation (papillary muscle, endocarditis), acute VSD, stress echocardiography, cardiac output assessment, 3D echocardiography, strain imaging, constrictive vs restrictive differentiation.

**Key Topics:**
- EF measurement methods
- Wall motion abnormality localization
- Diastolic dysfunction patterns
- Valve severity grading
- RV pressure estimation from TR velocity
- Tamponade physiology on echo
- Prosthetic valve assessment
- Strain imaging and GLS
- HFpEF diagnosis
- Stress echo safety and indications
