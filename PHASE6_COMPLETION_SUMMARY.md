## Module-Specific Highlights

### Vascular Surgery (40 units)
Comprehensive coverage of time-critical vascular emergencies: aortic dissection (Stanford classification, hypertension management), AAA rupture (permissive hypotension strategy), acute mesenteric ischemia (lactate significance, timing to revascularization), acute limb ischemia (Rutherford classification, thrombolysis vs surgery), vertebrobasilar insufficiency, carotid disease perioperative risk.

### Neurosurgical Emergencies (40 units)
Critical intracranial pathology: epidural/subdural hematoma (lens-shaped vs crescent imaging), SAH (Hunt-Hess grading, vasospasm management, nimodipine protocol), decompressive craniectomy outcomes, traumatic brain injury severity, spinal cord injury (ASIA scale), brain herniation syndromes (uncal, central, tonsillar), elevated ICP management.

### Cardiothoracic Surgery (40 units)
Perioperative cardiac complications: MI risk stratification (RCRI), sternal wound infections (mediastinitis urgency), post-op atrial fibrillation management, acute aortic insufficiency (vasodilators, NO iabp), LVAD complications (thrombosis, infection, RV failure), cardiac transplant rejection (acute vs chronic), pulmonary hypertension perioperatively.

### Trauma Surgery (50 units)
Damage control paradigm: abbreviated laparotomy philosophy, penetrating vs blunt decision trees, massive transfusion protocol (1:1:1 ratios), blast injuries (pressure wave mechanisms), crush syndrome (myoglobin management), compartment syndrome (pain on passive stretch, fasciotomy urgency), burn management (Parkland formula), hypothermia (appearance dead but not until warm and dead), tension pneumothorax (needle decompression at 2ICS), pericardial tamponade (Beck's triad, pericardiocentesis).

### Rare Diagnoses (60 units)
Medical zebras with high mortality if missed: TTP/HUS distinction (ADAMTS13 deficiency vs Shiga toxin), antiphospholipid syndrome (thrombosis + pregnancy complications), CAPS (multiple simultaneous thromboses), Sweet's syndrome (neutrophilic dermatosis), DRESS (drug reaction + eosinophilia + systemic), Kawasaki disease (coronary aneurysms in children), Takotsubo (stress cardiomyopathy with apical ballooning), Kounis (allergic MI), peripartum cardiomyopathy, giant cell myocarditis (fulminant).

### Infectious Disease (60 units)
Serious, sometimes overlooked infections: endocarditis (Modified Duke criteria, vegetation size), necrotizing fasciitis (pain > exam findings, time-critical fasciotomy), toxic shock (streptococcal vs staphylococcal), leptospirosis (biphasic illness, pulmonary hemorrhage), malaria (falciparum cerebral malaria, AKI), dengue (hemorrhagic fever, plasma leakage syndrome), Lyme (carditis, neuroborreliosis, CN VII palsy), atypical pneumonias (Legionella complications), prion diseases (CJD, vCJD, rapidly progressive).

### Hematology/Oncology (50 units)
Critical oncologic emergencies: tumor lysis syndrome (prevention with rasburicase, acute management with hyperkalemia), transfusion reactions (acute hemolytic, TRALI), TMA (malignancy-related microangiopathy), SVC syndrome (airway obstruction urgency), spinal cord compression (irreversible if delayed >48h), malignant tamponade (intrapericardial chemotherapy), febrile neutropenia (immediate broad-spectrum antibiotics), DIC (bleeding + clotting paradox).

### Advanced Toxicology (50 units)
Neurotoxins and critical overdoses: organophosphate cholinergic crisis (pralidoxime window), anticholinergic toxidrome (red as beet, dry as bone), serotonin syndrome (inducible clonus, SSRI/MAOI), NMS (lead pipe rigidity, dantrolene), sympathomimetic toxicity (cocaine/methamphetamine arrhythmias), hallucinogens (PCP rhabdomyolysis, LSD terror), sedative withdrawal (seizures, cardiovascular collapse), novel psychoactive substances (synthetic cannabinoids > THC potency), strychnine (opisthotonus), botulism (descending paralysis).

### Clinical Edge Cases (60 units)
Diagnostic pearls and presentations that mimic other conditions: occult shock (source unknown initially), paradoxical embolism (PFO + cryptogenic stroke), PRES (hypertensive breakthrough), RCVS (recurrent thunderclap headaches), SJS/TEN (epidermal necrolysis, drug-triggered), Charcot's triad (nystagmus + tremor + scanning speech), sentinel bleed (warning SAH), septic emboli (stroke from endocarditis), pituitary apoplexy (vision loss emergency), pediatric HUS (diarrhea-associated recovery-prone).
