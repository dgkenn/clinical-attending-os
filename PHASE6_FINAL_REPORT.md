- Epidural hematoma (lens-shaped imaging, lucid interval, emergency evacuation)
- Subdural hematoma (acute vs chronic, anticoagulation complications)
- Subarachnoid hemorrhage (Hunt-Hess grading, vasospasm management, nimodipine)
- Decompressive craniectomy (indications, outcomes, DECRA/RESCUEicp trials)
- Traumatic brain injury (GCS classification, DAI pathophysiology)
- Spinal cord injury (ASIA scale, neurogenic shock, neuroprotection)
- Brain herniation syndromes (uncal, central, tonsillar, Cushing triad)

#### Module 3: Cardiothoracic Surgery (40 units)
- Perioperative myocardial infarction (RCRI risk stratification, Type 1 vs Type 2)
- Sternal wound infection (mediastinitis emergency, debridement urgency)
- Atrial fibrillation post-cardiac surgery (rate control, anticoagulation)
- Acute aortic insufficiency (vasodilators, NO IABP, urgent surgery)
- LVAD complications (thrombosis, infection, RV failure management)
- Cardiac transplant rejection (acute vs chronic, immunosuppression)
- Pulmonary hypertension perioperatively (inhaled NO, cautious fluid management)
- CABG indications and outcomes (internal mammary patency, graft longevity)

#### Module 4: Trauma Surgery (50 units)
- Damage control surgery (abbreviated laparotomy, pack and return philosophy)
- Penetrating vs blunt trauma (decision algorithms, exploratory laparotomy)
- Massive transfusion protocol (1:1:1 PRBC:FFP:platelets, restrictive approach)
- Blast injuries (primary/secondary/tertiary/quaternary mechanisms)
- Crush syndrome (rhabdomyolysis, myoglobinuria, hyperkalemia, dialysis)
- Compartment syndrome (pain on passive stretch, fasciotomy urgency, 30 mmHg threshold)
- Burn injuries (Rule of 9's, Parkland formula, early excision/grafting)
- Hypothermia (perioperative management, ECMO rewarming, permissive)
- Tension pneumothorax (needle decompression 2ICS, diagnostic whoosh)
- Pericardial tamponade (Beck's triad, pericardiocentesis, subxiphoid approach)

#### Module 5: Rare Diagnoses & Zebras (60 units)
- Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (ADAMTS13 deficiency, plasma exchange)
- Hemolytic uremic syndrome (Shiga toxin, pediatric diarrhea-associated)
- Antiphospholipid syndrome (thrombosis + pregnancy complications)
- Catastrophic APS (multiple simultaneous thromboses, plasma exchange)
- Sweet's syndrome (acute febrile neutrophilic dermatosis)
- DRESS syndrome (drug reaction + eosinophilia + systemic symptoms)
- Kawasaki disease (coronary artery aneurysms, IVIG + aspirin protocol)
- Takotsubo cardiomyopathy (stress-induced, apical ballooning, recovery)
- Kounis syndrome (allergic myocardial infarction, vasospasm, avoid beta-blockers)
- Peripartum cardiomyopathy (pregnancy-associated HF, bromocriptine)
- Giant cell myocarditis (fulminant, immunosuppression, transplantation)

#### Module 6: Infectious Disease Deep Dive (60 units)
- Endocarditis (Modified Duke criteria, blood cultures, TEE, vegetation size)
- Necrotizing fasciitis (Type I/II/III, pain > exam, time-critical debridement)
- Toxic shock syndrome (streptococcal vs staphylococcal, exotoxin-mediated)
- Leptospirosis (biphasic illness, pulmonary hemorrhage, Weil disease)
- Malaria (falciparum cerebral malaria, AKI, artemisinin derivatives)
- Dengue fever (hemorrhagic fever, plasma leakage, DSS)
- Lyme disease (Lyme carditis, neuroborreliosis, AVB management)
- Atypical pneumonias (Legionella, Mycoplasma, Chlamydia, fluoroquinolone coverage)
- Prion diseases (CJD, vCJD, rapidly progressive dementia, fatal)

#### Module 7: Hematology/Oncology Emergencies (50 units)
- Tumor lysis syndrome (prevention with rasburicase, acute K/phosphate management)
- Acute hemolytic transfusion reaction (incompatible blood, hemoglobinuria, STOP transfusion)
- Febrile transfusion reaction vs TRALI (cytokines vs HLA antibodies)
- Thrombotic microangiopathy (malignancy-related, DIC distinction)
- Superior vena cava syndrome (airway obstruction risk, stent vs chemo)
- Spinal cord compression (irreversible if >48h, urgent decompression)
- Pericardial effusion/tamponade (intrapericardial chemotherapy, window)
- Febrile neutropenia (immediate broad-spectrum antibiotics, risk stratification)
- Disseminated intravascular coagulation (bleeding + clotting, treat cause)

#### Module 8: Advanced Toxicology (50 units)
- Organophosphate poisoning (cholinergic crisis, pralidoxime, atropine titration)
- Anticholinergic toxidrome (red/hot/dry/blind/mad, atropine/diphenhydramine, physostigmine)
- Serotonin syndrome (SSRI/MAOI, inducible clonus, hyperthermia, cyproheptadine)
- Neuroleptic malignant syndrome (antipsychotics, lead pipe rigidity, dantrolene)
- Sympathomimetic toxicity (cocaine/methamphetamine, arrhythmias, alpha blockers)
- Hallucinogen toxicity (PCP/LSD/psilocybin, psychosis, dissociation)
- Sedative-hypnotic withdrawal (benzodiazepine/barbiturate, seizures, taper protocol)
- Novel psychoactive substances (synthetic cannabinoids > THC potency, cathinones)
- Strychnine poisoning (glycine antagonist, opisthotonus, seizures, benzodiazepines)
- Botulism (descending paralysis, botulism immune globulin, supportive)

#### Module 9: Clinical Edge Cases & Pearls (60 units)
- Occult shock (source unknown, lactate-driven resuscitation)
- Paradoxical embolism (PFO + cryptogenic stroke, bubble study, closure decision)
- Posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (hypertensive, edema, seizures, reversible)
- Reversible cerebral vasoconstriction syndrome (thunderclap headaches, vasculature normalizes)
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome / Toxic epidermal necrolysis (drug-triggered, epidermal necrosis)
- Charcot's triad (nystagmus + tremor + scanning speech = MS demyelination)
- Sentinel bleed (warning SAH days before major, missed diagnosis risk)
- Septic emboli (endocarditis sheds infected particles, multiple infarcts)
- Pituitary apoplexy (hemorrhage into adenoma, thunderclap, visual loss, urgent surgery)
- Pediatric HUS (diarrhea-associated, better prognosis than adult, avoid antibiotics)
